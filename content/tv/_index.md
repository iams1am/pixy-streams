Long-form stories, season by season. This catalogue follows what is trending, the
highest-rated series and the shows currently on the air, with season and episode
details from TMDB where available.

Every series page is a discovery entry. It lists the official trailer, the
services that hold the rights and a season guide, so you can decide what to start
without hunting through five different apps. Episode lists are loaded for a
small, bounded selection and expand as the catalogue is refreshed.
