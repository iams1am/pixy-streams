The next fixtures and the last results from the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A,
Bundesliga, the Champions League, the NBA and Formula 1, pulled from public
scoreboards on every build.

Kick-off times are stored in UTC and converted to your device's local time when
JavaScript is available. Schedules, broadcasters and score states shift, so treat
the page as a guide and check the official competition or broadcaster before you
make plans around it.

**Live scores are not live video.** Pixy Streams reports a score state from the
last catalogue refresh. It does not host, mirror or link to live sports streams.
Broadcaster names are shown for reference only; rights and availability vary by
region.
