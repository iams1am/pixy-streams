What is Pixy Streams?

Pixy Streams is a single place to find something to watch. Browse what is trending in film and television, follow the anime seasons as they air, and keep up with the sport you actually care about - without hopping between five different apps to work out what is on.

Every title page gives you the essentials: the community rating, genres, runtime, the official trailer, and a direct link to the service that holds the rights to it. We point you to the right door instead of pretending to be the cinema.
